Data Economics: Geopolitical Stress & Market Resilience (2025-2026)

Python | Econometrics | Machine Learning
This project investigates the impact of the 2026 Israel-Iran conflict on the Brazilian market, challenging the common narrative of a new structural break. By combining Dimensionality Reduction (PCA) and Regime Identification (K-Means Clustering) with classical structural break diagnostics (Zivot-Andrews and Markov Switching Models), the analysis demonstrates that the market's underlying physics shifted in April 2025. The 2026 events represent a continuation of this high-stress, high-commodity regime rather than a new baseline.
