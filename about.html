About & Professional Manifesto

The Journey of Reinvention

Hi, I’m Itaiguara Bezerra. I am a Brazilian Economist, I hold an MSc from Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV-EPGE), and I am 56 years old.

For a long period, I navigated what many in our field fear the most: an involuntary status away from the stable job market. It was a period of silence, but not of standing still. In an industry that often overlooks experience in favor of youth, I chose a different path: absolute reinvention.

In October 2025, I began my journey with Python. In the beginning, it was a profound clash between decades of accumulated economic intuition and a new language of code. However, by leveraging AI as an advanced pedagogical framework, I unlocked the key to my personal breakthrough—allowing me to focus strictly on what matters: the logic, mathematical constraints, and economic intelligence behind the script.

Bridging the Dangerous Gap

Why am I sharing this? Because I identified a dangerous gap in our industry. On one side, we have solid, structural classical Economics; on the other, the fast-paced, unconstrained world of Data Science and Machine Learning.

"Too often, I see 'fancy scripts' designed solely to minimize error metrics like RMSE, while completely ignoring economic plausibility. A model that is statistically perfect but economically silent is useless for real-world decision-making."

The Data Economist

I call myself a Data Economist. I am not sure if this career officially exists yet, but it is exactly who I am. I bridge the technical divide by ensuring that advanced data science tools and algorithmic architectures are always guided and constrained by structural economic theory.

Through this platform, I share a series of rigorous macroeconomic, econometric, and machine learning exercises. I am mapping this trajectory to demonstrate that technical integrity, mathematical discipline, and the courage to reinvent oneself have no expiration date.
