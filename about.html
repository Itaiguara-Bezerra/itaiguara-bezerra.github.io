About Me

Sophistication of Expertise

Hi, I’m Itaiguara Bezerra. I am a Senior Quantitative Economist holding an MSc from the prestigious Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV-EPGE).

Throughout my career, I have navigated complex macroeconomic cycles, modeled high-stakes indicators, and deeply understood market realities. In an industry that frequently prioritizes superficial tech trends over deep structural knowledge, I chose to expand my boundaries not by abandoning my foundations, but by executing a deliberate technological sophistication of my analytical core.

By integrating modern statistical programming ecosystems like Python and R directly into my macroeconomic framework, I translated decades of economic intuition into scalable computation. Leveraging cutting-edge workflows allows me to bypass repetitive data-wrangling pipelines and focus strictly on what transforms data into strategy: structural economic logic, mathematical constraints, and model interpretability.

Bridging the Dangerous Gap

This professional pivot stems from a clear diagnosis of a critical flaw in the market. Today, a dangerous disconnect exists within industry analytics. On one side, classical macroeconomists manually process rigid models; on the other, data scientists deploy massive, unconstrained Machine Learning architectures completely detached from real-world logic.

"Too often, data pipelines are designed solely to optimize arbitrary error metrics like RMSE, while completely ignoring economic plausibility. A machine learning model that is statistically flawless but economically silent is structurally useless for institutional decision-making."

Quantitative Practice & Philosophy

To bridge this technical divide, I define my applied approach as that of a Data Economist—an economist who leverages code and quantitative methodologies as native extensions of economic theory. My objective is to ensure that advanced algorithmic frameworks, tree-based models, and dynamic econometric architectures remain strictly guided and constrained by macroeconomic reality.

This platform documents a rigorous repository of applied macroeconomic, econometric, and non-linear data science exercises. It serves as tangible proof that technical integrity, mathematical discipline, and a commitment to constant professional evolution are assets that only grow more robust with experience.
