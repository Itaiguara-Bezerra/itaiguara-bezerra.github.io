About & Professional Philosophy

The Data Economist DNA

I am a Senior Economist with a Master’s degree from Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV-EPGE), specialized in capturing structural macroeconomic trends through advanced computational modeling. My work bridges the gap between classical economic theory and non-linear data science pipelines. I believe that economic intuition must provide the foundational questions and structural constraints, while modern data science provides the precise computational scalpel to isolate true signal from market noise.

Project Manifesto: Geopolitical Stress & Market Resilience

To understand this philosophy in practice, look at my recent research regarding the 2026 Israel-Iran conflict and its transmission to the Brazilian market. While traditional narratives rushed to declare a "new structural break" or a brand-new crisis environment, the rigorous deployment of quantitative tools revealed a completely different physics under the hood.

1. No Data Slashing: This analysis rejects the standard practice of over-differentiating macro series (.diff()). Preserving the memory of the system in economic levels allows us to map the true long-run cointegration and structural transitions of the market.

2. Latent Risk DNA: By combining Linear Dimensionality Reduction (PCA) with Unsupervised Clustering (K-Means), we extract the latent risk neighborhoods of the market. The statistical DNA reveals that the "physics" of the current market actually shifted back in April 2025.

3. Antifragility in High-Stress: Validation via Markov Switching Models confirms that the 2026 shock did not cause a new regime shift. As a major commodity exporter with dollarized corporate hedges (e.g., Petrobras), the Brazilian market proved to be antifragile — it was already structurally built and priced for the chaos established a year prior.

This framework of challenging mainstream financial heuristics through strict mathematical and economic validation is what defines my pipeline. I prefer a real and bitter statistical result over a sweet, poorly modeled lie.
