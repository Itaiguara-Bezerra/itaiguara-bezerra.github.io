VIX Tupiniquim: A Modern Quantitative Framework for Measuring Brazilian Financial Stress

Author's Note: The term "Tupiniquim" is intentionally preserved as a Brazilian cultural expression used to denote something uniquely national. Throughout this project, it serves as the original brand identifier for a family of financial stress metrics specifically designed for the institutional, macroeconomic, and financial dynamics of Brazil.

Methodological Lenses Comparison

Methodological Lens Economic Question Quantitative Tool Core Contribution
Part I: Linear & Sparse How to condense a vast set of risk indicators into a single parsimonious metric? PCA + LASSO Extracts latent market stress while enforcing sparsity through L1 regularization.
Part II: Non-Linear How do non-proportional shocks and threshold effects impact financial stress? XGBoost + SHAP Captures complex interactions without sacrificing economic interpretability.
Part III: Time-Varying How do structural shifts and changing regimes alter stress propagation over time? TVP-VAR Models dynamic parameter shifts and structural breaks across economic crises.
