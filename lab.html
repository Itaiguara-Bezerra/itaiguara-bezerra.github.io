Methodological Lenses Comparison

Methodological Lens Economic Question Quantitative Tool Core Contribution
Part I: Linear & Regularized How can a broad set of macro-financial indicators be condensed and selected into a parsimonious measure of financial stress? PCA + LASSO + Ridge + Elastic Net Condenses common variation through PCA and applies linear regularization to promote parsimonious variable selection and mitigate multicollinearity.
Part II: Non-Linear How do nonlinear relationships and interactions among risk indicators shape financial stress? XGBoost + SHAP Captures nonlinear relationships and interactions while providing an interpretable framework for understanding variable contributions.
Part III: Time-Varying How do relationships among economic variables evolve as financial stress changes over time? TVP-VAR-SV Allows model parameters and residual volatility to evolve over time, providing a Bayesian framework for dynamic variance decomposition (FEVD).
Research Perspective: The objective is not to identify a universally superior model, but to examine how different quantitative lenses reveal different dimensions of the same economic phenomenon.
